# Platinum Silicone Guide and Tricks

This repository is the product of my frustration trying to find resources about platinum silicone part of my hobby sculpting projects, finally decided to create this so people can learn from mine (and hopefully others mistakes) of what works and what doesn't.

This does not contain basic information like what platinum silicone is, how to process it, degassing, etc... none of that, this goes mostly into the depths of tests that were tried for more obscure subjects.

Hopefully reading this guide will provide you with greater understanding of what is and isn't possible without having to do a lot of tests.

## Table Of Reference

1. Compatible Chemical Processes.
  - Cyanoacrylate processes.
  - Epoxies (Plastic Weld, Steel Weld, XTC-3D coats).
  - Silicone Sealant processes (Transparent, White).
  - Mineral Spirits.
  - Xylene.
  - Novocs.
3. Compatible 3D Printed Mold Processes (Or Moldmaking Processes).
  - PLA.
  - PETG.
  - ABS + Acetone.
  - Polysmooth + Isopropanol.
4. Gluing.
  - Gluing between plat silicone.
  - Coloring.
  - Gluing to plastic.
  - Gluing to metal/glass.
  - Gluing to fabric.
  - Gluing to tin silicone.
  - Mechanical direct gluing.
6. Reinforcing plat silicone and changing its properties.
  - Modacrylic fibers.
  - Cloth.
7. Cheating cure inhibition (mineral spirits + sealant + hot air).
8. Fur and hair.
  - 4 way strech silicone fur chunks.
  - Hand layed fur and hair.
  - Needle Punching.
  - Direct laying, broken backing, and other simpler methods.
9. Cheap Flocking.
  - Standard.
  - High Resistance.
10. High gloss paint on soft tacky silicone.
